🎓 Scholarship Outreach Management System

Product Summary & Feature Specification

📌 System Summary

The Scholarship Outreach Management System is a web-based productivity platform designed to help students efficiently manage their professor outreach and scholarship application process.

Students applying for fully funded programs often contact dozens of professors, track multiple deadlines, send repeated follow-ups, and prepare different documents. Managing all of this manually through spreadsheets and emails leads to confusion, missed opportunities, and poor organization.

This system centralizes the entire workflow into one intelligent dashboard. It enables students to organize professor contacts, generate professional emails, schedule follow-ups, track replies, manage application deadlines, store notes, and monitor outreach progress — all without requiring complex email integrations.

The platform acts as a personal scholarship assistant, helping students stay consistent, organized, and strategic throughout their application journey. Instead of remembering everything manually, students receive reminders, visual progress tracking, and structured workflows that save time and reduce stress.

The ultimate goal of the system is to increase students’ response rates, reduce outreach mistakes, and improve overall scholarship success.

⚙ Core System Features (MVP)
1. User Account & Profile Management

This feature allows students to create a personalized workspace.

Students can register using email and password and set up their profile by selecting their target degree (MS, PhD, Research Assistant), research interests, preferred destination countries, and academic background. They can upload their CV and basic academic details so that the system can personalize email templates automatically.

Each user gets a private dashboard where all outreach activities are stored securely.

2. Professor & University Contact Manager

This is the core tracking engine of the platform.

Students can add professors manually by entering information such as professor name, university, department, email address, research area, country, and application deadline.

Each professor entry becomes a structured profile card inside the dashboard. This eliminates scattered spreadsheets and allows students to see all contacts in one organized view.

The system also supports tagging by research field (such as AI, Law, Biology, Data Science), making it easier to group professors based on specialization.

3. Outreach Status Pipeline System

This feature provides a visual progress tracker.

Each professor contact moves through predefined stages such as:

Draft

Email Sent

Replied

Follow-up Needed

Interview Scheduled

Accepted

Rejected

Students can easily update the status with one click. This visual pipeline gives a clear overview of current progress and helps prioritize daily tasks.

Unlike spreadsheets, this system allows instant filtering and status-based tracking.

4. Professional Email Template System

The platform includes ready-to-use professional outreach email templates.

Students can choose templates based on their goal, such as PhD funding inquiry, MS scholarship request, research assistant outreach, or follow-up email.

Templates support dynamic personalization. The system automatically inserts professor name, university name, research area, and student information. Students only need to customize a few lines before sending.

This feature saves time and improves email quality, especially for beginners who struggle with writing formal academic emails.

5. Manual Email Sending Assistance

To keep the MVP simple and secure, the platform provides a smart email sending assistant instead of direct Gmail integration.

With one click, students can copy the generated email content and open it in their default email client with subject and body pre-filled. After sending, they can mark the email as sent inside the dashboard.

The system automatically records the sending date and updates the status to “Email Sent”.

This approach avoids technical complexity while still providing automation benefits.

6. Follow-Up Reminder System

This feature prevents missed opportunities.

Students can set follow-up reminders for each professor, such as after 7 days or 14 days. If no reply is received, the system sends reminder notifications.

The dashboard highlights contacts that require follow-up, ensuring students maintain consistent communication.

This structured reminder system increases reply rates and improves professionalism.

7. Deadline & Application Tracking

The platform helps students manage application deadlines efficiently.

Students can add deadlines for each university or professor. The system displays upcoming deadlines and sends alerts before due dates.

A document checklist is included where students can track required materials such as CV, SOP, research proposal, transcripts, IELTS scores, and recommendation letters.

This reduces last-minute stress and helps students stay prepared.

8. Notes & Communication Log

Each professor profile includes a private notes section.

Students can record:

Reply summaries

Interview feedback

Requested documents

Professor comments

Next steps

This creates a personal communication history that helps students stay organized throughout long application cycles.

9. Search, Filter & Smart Sorting

The system allows fast navigation across large contact lists.

Students can search by professor name, university, country, or research area. They can filter contacts based on status, deadline, or response type.

Sorting options allow students to prioritize urgent tasks and focus on high-interest opportunities.

10. Progress Analytics Dashboard

This feature provides performance insights.

Students can view:

Total emails sent

Number of replies

Positive response count

Interview invitations

Success rate percentage

Visual statistics motivate students to maintain consistency and optimize outreach strategies.

11. Data Export & Backup

Students can export their outreach data in Excel or CSV format for backup or offline use.

This ensures data ownership and transparency while building trust in the platform.

🎯 System Value Proposition

This system transforms chaotic scholarship outreach into a structured workflow.

It replaces spreadsheets with automation, replaces memory with reminders, replaces confusion with clarity, and replaces stress with control.

Instead of working harder, students work smarter.
